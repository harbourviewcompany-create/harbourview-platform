# Talent Matching and Eligibility

Anchors: TAL-065–068, TAL-027, TAL-041–049, TAL-095; TAC-024,025,043.

Matching separates hard eligibility from required capabilities, preferred capabilities and preferences/context. Missing evidence is not automatically failure.

Factor result vocabulary: `satisfied`, `not_satisfied`, `unresolved`, `not_applicable`.

Every stored match records policy version, job/requirement version, Passport/input snapshot hash, credential effective state, taxonomy version, eligibility-rule version, factors/weights, evidence references, missing evidence and timestamp.

Invalidation occurs on relevant job/requirement, Passport assertion, credential state, taxonomy mapping, privacy/visibility, eligibility-rule or match-policy change. Historical runs remain reproducible.

Semantic similarity may improve retrieval/ranking but cannot invent professional or legal eligibility. Authority-backed eligibility adapters are activated only where an authoritative contract exists.
