# Delivery Roadmap

## Phase 0 — Constitution and foundation

Global jurisdiction registry, source taxonomy, ontology, provenance, source rights, data classification, threat model, ADRs and reviewer authorities.

## Phase 1 — Evidence and regulatory spine

Source operations, immutable snapshots, parsing, citation anchors, versions, diffs, regulatory instruments/provisions, search, analyst review, publication and correction.

## Phase 2 — Applicability and obligations

Organization profiles, DSL, obligation extraction/review, controls, deadlines, alerts, tasks, assessments, evidence and remediation.

## Phase 3 — Entities, licences and quality

Legal entities, ownership, facilities, licences, certifications, inspections, enforcement, recalls, counterparty verification and monitoring.

## Phase 4 — Market access and corridors

Product classification, origin/transit/destination routes, gates, permits, customs, quality, market-entry projects, determinations and deal rooms.

## Phase 5 — Market intelligence

Metric registry, official/licensed datasets, reconciliation, sales, pricing, inventory, capacity, demand, tenders, forecasts and scenarios.

## Phase 6 — Transaction layer

Qualified partner discovery, requests, conditions, document exchange, readiness, shipment milestones, performance history and lawful fee capability.

## Phase 7 — Global institutional depth

More languages, local rules, government deployments, private regions, advanced forecasting and continuous coverage assurance.
