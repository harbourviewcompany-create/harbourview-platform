# Scope Ledger

## Authorized objective

Create the complete technical foundation for a global cannabis regulatory intelligence and market-entry operating system covering evidence, regulatory change, applicability, obligations, compliance controls, licensing, facilities, quality, counterparties, trade corridors, market data, alerts, APIs, analyst review, governance, commercial entitlements and controlled transaction execution.

## Global universe

The canonical jurisdiction model must represent international treaties and standards; supranational unions and customs blocs; all sovereign countries; dependent territories and separately regulated territories; national, federal, state, provincial, territorial, regional, municipal, tribal, Indigenous, autonomous and special-zone authorities; courts, regulators, licensing programmes, reimbursement systems; and origin/transit/destination corridor combinations.

The source universe includes binding law, legislative material, regulations, gazettes, agency guidance, court and tribunal decisions, enforcement, recalls, licence registers, corporate and beneficial-ownership registries, facility and quality records, customs and tax sources, official market data, licensed commercial feeds, company filings, tenders and secondary-source leads.

The product/activity universe includes medical, pharmaceutical, scientific, adult-use, hemp, cannabinoid and industrial regimes; plant material, APIs, intermediates, extracts, finished products and waste; and cultivation, manufacture, testing, packaging, storage, transport, wholesale, retail, prescribing, dispensing, import, export, transit and destruction.

## Scope-preservation rule

No phase, first market, budget, customer segment, source-access limitation or implementation shortcut removes an element from the target state. Temporary absences must appear as explicit coverage states, dependencies, entitlements or rights constraints.

## Excluded autonomous authority

The platform cannot autonomously issue legal advice, grant licences or permits, authorize customs release or shipments, approve regulated transactions, represent unsourced inference as law, override customer authority matrices or publish high-risk conclusions without required review.
