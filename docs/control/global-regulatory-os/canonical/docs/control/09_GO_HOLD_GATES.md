# GO/HOLD Gates

## Architecture

GO when scope, ontology, provenance, source rights, public/private boundaries and reviewer authority are approved. HOLD when any can be bypassed or remains materially ambiguous.

## Database

GO when clean install and supported upgrades pass, RLS matrix is green and history is preserved. HOLD for cross-tenant access, request-path owner roles or destructive migration without recovery.

## Source

GO when rights, schedules, identifiers, parsers, health, freshness and fallback are defined. HOLD for rights uncertainty, silent failures or stale critical evidence without warning.

## Publication

GO when every claim has current evidence, effective-date treatment, approval and an allowlisted projection. HOLD for unsupported, conflicting, stale or unreviewed high-risk claims.

## AI

GO when model, prompt, data classes, tools, evaluations, approval and rollback are documented. HOLD when high-risk output can commit publication or durable effects without fresh authorization.

## Applicability

GO when inputs, rules, evidence, assumptions and reasons are reproducible and critical false-negative tests pass. HOLD when hidden inference or missing profiles produce definitive answers.

## Corridor

GO when product, purpose, parties, route, date, quantity, permits, quality, customs and transit are complete. HOLD when a country-pair summary is represented as shipment readiness or authorization.

## Market data

GO when definitions, methods, units, revisions and forecast baselines are explicit and reconciled. HOLD for unexplained conflicts or unsourced estimates presented as reported facts.

## Security

GO when no high/critical findings remain and tenant isolation, leakage, audit, backups and incident controls pass. HOLD for unauthorized access, leakage, missing restore proof or residency breach.

## Commercial

GO when all product surfaces and checkout use one plan/entitlement catalogue. HOLD for conflicting prices, tiers, jurisdictions, modules or limits.

## Production

GO only with complete release evidence, approved migrations, monitoring/on-call, rollback or forward-fix proof and product-owner authorization.
