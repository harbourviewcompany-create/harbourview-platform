# Data Governance and Provenance

## Ownership

- Data Governance Lead: canonical definitions, quality and corrections.
- Source Operations: access, rights, schedules and health.
- Regulatory Editorial: factual and interpretive publication.
- Legal Review: high-risk legal interpretations within authorized jurisdictions.
- Market Methodology Owners: metric definitions, estimates and forecasts.
- Privacy and Security: classification, retention, residency and access.

## Quality dimensions

Accuracy, completeness, timeliness, consistency, validity, uniqueness, traceability, interpretability, rights compliance and reproducibility.

## Record states

Draft, candidate, under review, approved, published, superseded, corrected, retracted, archived and rejected.

## Conflict policy

When authoritative sources conflict, preserve each source separately, establish authority hierarchy and dates, block automatic canonical selection, assign specialist review, publish material uncertainty and never average legal facts. Market metrics may be reconciled only under a versioned methodology.

## Freshness

Every published record exposes source publication date, effective date, retrieval date, last verification, next review, freshness SLA and one of: current, review due, stale, source unavailable, superseded or historical.

## Corrections

A correction records the affected publication versions, error category, materiality, corrected evidence, reviewer, approval, affected tenants/exports, notification requirement and preventive action. Material corrections regenerate dependent reports and applicability determinations.
