# ADR-009: Human Authority for High Risk

**Status:** Accepted  
**Date:** 2026-07-31

## Decision

AI creates candidates; authorized people approve high-risk obligations, interpretations, corridors and transaction eligibility.

## Consequences

Protects against unsupported legal and regulated decisions.

## Rejected alternative

Autonomous publication or authorization was rejected.

## Verification

The relevant contracts, migration tests, authorization tests, lineage tests or operational evidence must be attached to the phase release record.
