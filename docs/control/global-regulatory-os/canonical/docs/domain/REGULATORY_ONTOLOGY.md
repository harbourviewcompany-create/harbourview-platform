# Canonical Regulatory Ontology

## Rules

Canonical concepts use stable IDs, not display labels. Jurisdiction-specific terms map through versioned relationships: exact, broader, narrower, conditional, disputed or unmapped. Historical mappings remain effective-dated. Product, substance, activity, licence, authority, quality, obligation and commercial concepts remain separate dimensions.

## Concept families

### Legal and authority
Jurisdiction, authority, regulator, court, programme, instrument type, legal status, provision type, enforcement action and appeal route.

### Cannabis and substances
Cannabis plant, hemp, jurisdiction-specific marijuana class, cannabinoid, controlled substance, narcotic drug, psychotropic substance, precursor, API, intermediate, finished product, waste and destruction material.

### Products
Genetics/seeds, fresh/dried plant material, extract, isolate, oil, inhalable, edible, beverage, topical, oral dosage form, prescription medicine, investigational product, ingredient, consumer hemp product and industrial product.

### Intended uses
Medical, pharmaceutical, research, clinical trial, adult use, wellness, industrial, food, cosmetic, veterinary and destruction.

### Activities
Breeding, cultivation, harvest, processing, extraction, manufacture, packaging, labelling, testing, storage, transport, wholesale, distribution, retail, pharmacy dispensing, prescribing, administration, import, export, transit, destruction, research, advertising and reporting.

### Authorizations
Licence class, registration, permit, product authorization, facility authorization, controlled-drug authorization, import/export permit, customs authorization, certification and accreditation.

### Quality
GACP, GMP, GDP, GLP, GCP, QMS, pharmacopoeial standard, laboratory accreditation, batch release, stability, contaminant testing and recall.

### Obligation semantics
Required, prohibited, permitted, conditional, exempted, reporting, notification, payment, training, record retention and inspection readiness.

## Mapping record

Canonical concept, source jurisdiction, source term/language, evidence, relationship, conditions, effective dates, confidence, reviewer and status.

## Acceptance archetypes

Validate the ontology against: a federal/state adult-use system; a unitary medical system; a pharmaceutical/GMP import system; a hemp-specific regime; a prohibition/high-restriction regime; a supranational-plus-national system; and tribal, Indigenous or municipal overlays.
